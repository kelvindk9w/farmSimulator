## 1. Which 10 farm-themed games rank highest on the App Store today (US and Brazil; top grossing vs top free vs top paid vs Apple Arcade)?

### Takeaway
Township (Playrix) and Hay Day (Supercell) are clearly the two biggest farm games by revenue, with Township roughly three times Hay Day's size, and they are the only farm titles I could confirm in the US iPhone top-grossing games chart in early November 2026 (Township about #14, Hay Day about #66). Behind them the 2026 order has changed: two 2026 launches, Heartopia (a life sim with farming) and My Garden Tale, are now reported next to or ahead of the older "farm adventure" titles, while the paid farm tier is represented by Stardew Valley and the Farming Simulator mobile series. I could not retrieve a Brazil top-grossing chart, so the Brazil ordering is unconfirmed.

## 2. How does the iOS farming chart differ from Android, and what share of farming revenue comes from iOS?

### Takeaway
I found no title-level iOS versus Android revenue split for any farm game. The only supported points are market-wide: the App Store takes the larger share of store game revenue, and on iOS the genre competes in three separate tiers - free-to-play grossing, paid (Stardew Valley, Farming Simulator) and Apple Arcade (Stardew Valley+, Farmside, Wylde Flowers).

- If farm games follow the market-wide store split, iOS would contribute well over half of store revenue for the leaders while providing a minority of downloads; this is an assumption, not a measured figure for any farm title.

Whether the paid titles rank differently on Google Play was not checked (Android is out of scope here).
- For a Brazil-based studio, the Brazil iOS revenue pool for a single top farm game looks small: Hay Day's Brazil iOS weekly revenue of roughly $31K-$37.5K (2023-2024, section 1) implies roughly $130K-$160K a month for the genre's number two. This is an extrapolation from two old weekly data points.

### Inferences
- The two revenue leaders are more than a decade old and grow through event cadence rather than new core systems; a new game needs an event framework from launch, not as a later addition.
- Mini-games are already validated as a retention and monetisation layer inside farm games (Township's match-3 events; Farm It! on Arcade), which supports the client's plan to include mini-games.

### Gaps
- No sourced per-game breakdown of core loop, timers versus energy, meta systems, onboarding or art quality. **[UNVERIFIED]** prior-knowledge summary for the writer to confirm:
  - Hay Day: plant, harvest, feed animals, craft in production buildings, fulfil truck, boat and town orders; timers only, no energy; storage capacity (barn and silo) is the main friction; side areas include fishing, a town and a seasonal valley.
  - Township: farm plus city builder; crops feed factories, which feed helicopter, train and plane orders; zoo, mine and islands as side systems; match-3 levels as recurring events.
  - Family Island, Klondike Adventures, Family Farm Adventure: "farm adventure" format - a home farm for production plus story-driven expedition maps where clearing obstacles costs energy; time-limited event islands.
  - FarmVille 3: animal breeding and collection focus, farmhands with skills, weather affecting yields. FarmVille 2: Country Escape: 3D farm with offline play.
  - Stardew Valley: day-cycle farming RPG with stamina, seasons, relationships, mining, fishing and festivals; no timers tied to real time.
  - Farming Simulator mobile: realistic 3D machinery operation with licensed brands, field work, production chains and AI helpers.
  - Heartopia: 3D social life sim with farming, fishing, cooking, home building and shared towns.
  - Hello Kitty Island Adventure: friendship-driven island life sim with crafting and quests. Wylde Flowers: fully voiced narrative farm and witchcraft sim.

## 4. Monetization for each game, and how paid and Apple Arcade farm games succeed without IAP

### Takeaway
The free-to-play leaders pair a hard currency with a low-priced season pass (Township's Golden Ticket is $4.99 in the US), while the premium tier is priced at $4.99 to $7.99 upfront, and Farming Simulator 23 adds optional IAP on top. ## 7. What do App Store reviewers praise and complain about, and what gaps could a new game fill?

### Takeaway
The only sourced sentiment is that Township players find the premium currency scarce and that currency monetisation is a recurring complaint; ratings stay high regardless (4.7-4.8 for the leaders). Market data shows the audience growing again and a new social life sim (Heartopia) breaking into the top tier in 2026, which is encouraging for a new multiplayer farm game but also means a strong new reference competitor.

### Cited Findings
- Township: a reviewer states T-cash "is hard to come by", developer replies direct players to in-game ways of earning it — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/ph/app/township/id638689075); an audit flags T-cash monetisation as a recurring complaint — [Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/games/com-playrix-township-ios)

If the presentation attributes listed as unverified in section 1 hold (Heartopia 3D, Hay Day and Township 2D isometric), a 3D multiplayer farm with real-time friend play sits in the part of the market that is growing, though Heartopia is then a direct reference competitor.
