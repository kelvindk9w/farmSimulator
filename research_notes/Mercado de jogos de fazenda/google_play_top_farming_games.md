## 1. Which 10 farm-themed games rank highest on Google Play today, globally and in Brazil?

### Takeaway
Township (Playrix) and Hay Day (Supercell) are far ahead of everything else on every metric found (≈500M and ≈369M estimated Google Play installs; ≈$40–43M and ≈$13–16M estimated monthly revenue in 2026), and the only new entrant to reach their revenue tier is the 3D multiplayer life-sim Heartopia (XD, global launch January 2026; ~$67M in H1 2026). Below them sits a mid-tier of energy/expedition "farm adventure" games (Klondike Adventures, Family Island, Family Farm Adventure) and the Zynga FarmVille titles; the ranking of positions 4–10 is low-confidence and no dated Brazil chart could be retrieved.

## 2. For each game: core loop, meta systems, session design, mini-games

### Takeaway
The sourced evidence shows three distinct designs at the top: (a) the timer-based production-and-orders farm (Hay Day), (b) the farm/city tycoon that has become a shell around a match-3 engine and rotating mini-games (Township), and (c) the energy-gated exploration/expedition farm (Klondike Adventures, Family Island); Heartopia adds a fourth, fully 3D multiplayer life-sim. Detailed per-game system descriptions could not be source-verified in this session and are given only as flagged background.

### Cited Findings
- Township has gone through five eras over 17 years: from a Facebook Canvas farming sim, to a hyper-casual mini-game platform, to its current form in which "match-3 is the main engine and the tycoon is essentially the onboarding". — [25gamers / Two & a Half Gamers](https://25gamers.com/two-a-half-gamers-%F0%9F%92%B0%F0%9F%9A%9C-how-playrix-turned-a-17-year-old-tycoon-into-a-45m-month-ua-match-3-engine/); [GameRefinery](https://www.gamerefinery.com/how-playrix-turned-township-into-a-45m-month-engine-a-two-a-half-gamers-episode-with-gamerefinery/)
- Township's early events had very basic rules; over time it introduced high-production events that mimic the core mechanics of hit casual and hyper-casual games as mini-games. — [Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2020/10/13/how-playrix-township-became-a-billion-dollar-game)
- Township content cadence is described as "new seasons, adventures, collections, and events landing constantly". — [Glotier](https://glotier.com/games/township)
- Klondike Adventures is a farming simulator combined with adventure: players explore expedition lands, and collections are assembled from items found in treasures there. — [Fetch blog](https://fetch.com/blog/treat-yourself/earn-free-gift-cards-playing-klondike-adventures-with-fetch-play)

It has a "Candy Shop" mini-game with limited moves. — [Level Winner guide (2021)](https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=42425)
- Family Island "simulates life and everyday farming activities on a tropical island" while taking players on an adventure; energy is a bottleneck that players ask the developer to relax. — [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/family-island-monetization)
- Hay Day's Farm Pass grants season-long perks such as extra slots and spin events. — [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/hay-day-monetization)
- Hay Day's record day (1 May 2026) combined two collection events, task rewards and a linked exchange shop — i.e. collection/exchange meta layered on the farm loop. — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-sensor-towers-h1-analysis-hay-days-comeback-julys-top-games-big-krafton-numbers-and-more/)

### Inferences
The following per-game profiles are **[UNVERIFIED-BK]** — researcher background knowledge, no source retrieved this session. Use as a checklist to verify, not as citable fact.

- **Hay Day** — Loop: plant crops → harvest → feed animals / mill feed → craft goods in production buildings with queue slots → fulfil truck orders (coins + XP), boat orders (crates, friends can fill), town visitors → sell surplus player-to-player in the Roadside Shop. Session friction comes from timers (minutes to a day or more) and from **barn/silo capacity**, upgraded with scarce drop materials; there is no energy system. Meta: XP levels unlocking crops/machines, land expansion with rare materials, fishing lake, a separate Town, the Valley (seasonal shared map), decoration and farm customization, collections/stickers.
- **Township** — Loop: crops → factories → helicopter orders, trains (building materials), airplane crates → construct community buildings to raise population caps → unlock more factories and land. Meta: zoo, mine, islands/ships, town decoration, plus match-3 levels and rotating mini-game events that feed event progression. No energy for farming; match-3 uses lives.
- **Family Island** — Story-led (stone-age family), home island with crops, animals and workshops; energy is spent to clear obstacles and explore chains of temporary and permanent islands; time-limited expedition islands drive urgency.
- **Klondike Adventures** — Gold-rush setting; home station farm plus energy-gated expedition maps with story quests; temporary event lands.
- **Family Farm Adventure** — Same energy/expedition template with puzzle rooms and a story; farm supplies crafting inputs for exploration.
- **FarmVille 2: Country Escape** — Crops, animals, crafting workshops, an orders board, farmhands sent to forage locations, playable offline. **FarmVille 3** — animal-breeding focus, farmhands with skills, weather that affects yields.
- **Heartopia** — Fully 3D avatar-based town life: gardening/farming, fishing, cooking, bug catching, pets, home building in a shared online town.
- **Stardew Valley** — Premium farming RPG with day/season clock and stamina; **Farming Simulator** — realistic 3D machinery operation with licensed brands.
- Cross-cutting pattern (inference from the sourced items): none of the top earners relies on the bare plant→harvest→sell loop. Each wraps it in a second engine — player economy and co-op competition (Hay Day), match-3 and mini-games (Township), exploration and story (Klondike, Family Island), or avatar social life (Heartopia). A new 3D farm with mini-games and quests is aligned with this, but the mini-games need to be a real retention engine rather than decoration.
